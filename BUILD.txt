BUILD TAG: patient-home-treated-muscles
DATE: 2026-06-04
NEW MIGRATION: none (no DB changes)

WHAT THIS ADDS
--------------
On the PATIENT's own home page, a quiet "See which muscles were treated" button now
appears (below the goals, only when a treatment is on record). Tapping it opens a
read-only pop-up listing the muscles injected at the patient's most recent treatment,
with the side (left / right / both) and the treatment date.

It shows only the muscles - no doses, drug, or other clinical detail - matching the
"just the muscles for now" scope. Nothing is shown until the patient taps the button.

This builds on the previous slice (the per-goal "View graph" button).

WHAT CHANGED
------------
- app/[locale]/page.tsx                      - the button + the read-only pop-up
- components/cards/TreatedMusclesModal.tsx   - NEW read-only muscles pop-up
- lib/supabase/patientHome.ts                - also loads the last treatment's muscles
- messages/en.json, messages/da.json         - new patient.home strings (en + da)

No database, RPC, or migration changes. Reads use existing RLS policies
(treatment_session_patient_read / muscle_injection_select), which already let a patient
read their own treatment. Muscle grouping reuses the same helper as the clinician and
physiotherapist views.

DEPLOY STEPS
------------
1. Unzip and push the contents to your GitHub repo (replace the working tree).
2. Vercel builds & deploys automatically on push.
3. No migrations, no new environment variables.

HOW TO CONFIRM
--------------
1. Sign in as a patient who has a treatment on record (e.g. test1).
2. On the home screen, below the goals, you'll see "See which muscles were treated".
3. Tap it: a pop-up lists the treated muscles + side, with the treatment date. Close
   returns to the home screen. (If a patient has no treatment recorded, the button is
   hidden.)
