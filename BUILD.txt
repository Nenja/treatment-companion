BUILD: recorder-upload-clinic-overlay   (two follow-ups, NO migration)
=========================================================================
1) RECORDER FILE-UPLOAD FALLBACK
   The video recorder now offers "Attach a file" next to live recording:
   - On a clinic desktop without a webcam, attach a video you already have.
   - On a phone, the same control still offers the camera (capture attribute).
   - The option also appears on the camera-error screen and when the browser
     can't record at all.
   Picked files are checked (must be a video, max 200 MB), previewed, then kept
   through the same path as a recorded clip. This benefits both the patient
   check-in recorder and the clinician baseline recorder.

2) CLINIC vs PATIENT 0–10 ON THE NRS TREND
   For NRS goals, the clinic's 0–10 video score is now overlaid on the goal's
   own trend chart as a distinct dark dotted line with square markers — beside
   the patient's sage self-report line (and the amber physiotherapist line when
   present). A legend entry and a tap-to-read caption are included. GAS goals
   are unchanged (they keep their separate clinic trend chart).
