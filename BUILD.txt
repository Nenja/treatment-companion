WHAT'S NEW: A PICTURE OF EACH GOAL'S RESPONSE
You can now export a goal's response chart as an image to paste into the
record, the same idea as the face-map picture. ONE picture per goal.

Two places to get it:
1. ON EACH GOAL'S GRAPH — a small download icon next to the expand
   icon at the top right of the chart. Tap it to save that goal's PNG.
2. IN THE EXPORT DIALOG — under the text there's now a "Goal response
   charts" list with a PNG button for every goal (active and earlier
   ones).

The image is designed for a record: white background, dark ink, a fixed
width, with the goal text, the cycle, a legend, and a one-line summary
baked in so it stands alone. NRS goals show the 0-10 line with the
baseline and target marked; GAS goals show the five levels (much worse
... much better than expected). A skipped week shows as a gap; a dotted
line marks where the effect started wearing off.

The text export is unchanged. The picture is an extra option.
