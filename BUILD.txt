Build tag: clinician-training-overview

NEW: "Home training" overview on the clinician patient page
- A card at the top of the active-cycle goals section (above the goal
  graphs) showing the patient's training reports as a week × day grid:
  rows = Mon–Sun, columns = weeks left→right (same timeline as the goal
  charts), filled cell = trained that day. A summary line shows
  "avg X days/week · N total", and the current week is marked.
- Reads weekly_checkin.training_days for the ACTIVE cycle only (past
  cycles are not shown, as requested). Weeks with no reported training
  show empty; an empty report (checked in, trained no days) counts as a
  reported week in the average.
- The card appears once the patient has at least one check-in. If no
  training has been reported yet it shows "No training reported yet".

Includes the patient-side capture from the previous build (the check-in
training-days step) unchanged.

MIGRATIONS

  (previous build) applied — it adds weekly_checkin.training_days and the
