BUILD: action-row-tidy   (clinician patient-page menu + panels)
=========================================================================
NO MIGRATION — client-only. Nothing to run in Supabase.

WHAT THIS BUILD DOES
- Reordered the patient-page action icons: medication, training, therapist
  (physio), history, export. Applies to all layouts (body row, wide header
  toolbar, side rail).
- Made the therapist (physio) panel compact: smaller header and tighter goal /
  flagged-muscle rows — it was heavier than its content warranted.
- Panels now open FROM the menu: the action row + its panels (medication /
  therapist / training) moved to the top of the left context column, so an
  opened panel sits directly under the menu (under the header toolbar on wide,
  under the in-body row on narrow) instead of far down the column. The training
  icon opens the weekly training calendar as before.

DEPLOY
- Unzip; push to GitHub; Vercel builds on push. Page count 60. No SQL.
