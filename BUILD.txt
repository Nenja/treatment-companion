BUILD: goal-edit   (edit/recalibrate a goal — build 2a of goal versioning)
=========================================================================
NO MIGRATION — uses edit_goal from 0086. Ensure 0086 is applied.

WHAT THIS BUILD DOES
A clinician can recalibrate a goal at a visit. Each goal card on the clinician
patient page now has a "Recalibrate" button and a vN version label. It opens a
drawer pre-filled with the goal's current wording and calibration; saving calls
edit_goal, which creates the next version in the active cycle and freezes the
previous one. Past ratings stay bound to the version they were made under.

- NRS goals: edit wording + baseline/target.
- GAS goals: edit wording + the five anchors.
- Video task and therapy tag carry forward unchanged (the drawer says so). Cut
  points carry forward (not exposed for editing).

FILES
- lib/supabase/clinicianPatient.ts: ClinicianPatientGoal gains lineageId +
  version (added to the goal read); new useEditGoal hook.
- components/clinician/EditGoalDrawer.tsx: the recalibrate drawer.
- clinician patient page: Recalibrate button on bont + itb goal cards; drawer.

DEPLOY

** QA (please verify — can't be tested here) **
- Recalibrate a goal: the drawer is pre-filled with current values.
- Save: the card shows the new version (vN+1) with edited values; the old

- The goal's past chart points (under the old version) are unaffected.
