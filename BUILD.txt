WHAT CHANGED & WHY:
  Several pages showed the grey loading skeleton until data arrived. But if the data request FAILED
  (e.g. the column/migration problems we hit earlier), there was no "arrived" — so the skeleton
  stayed forever, with no error and no way to retry. That was the real reason pages "got stuck
  loading."
  Now: a failed load shows a small, calm "Something went wrong — Try again" panel (with a retry
  button) instead of an endless skeleton. Also added a safety net that catches unexpected crashes
  inside a page and shows the same panel rather than a blank screen. Nothing changes when things load
  normally.
