WHAT THIS BUILD ADDS (on top of audit-fixes)
The EHR-paste export (lib/ehrExport.ts) is now fully localised. Before, only
the dates followed the app locale; every label and sentence fragment was
hardcoded English. Now:
- buildEhrExport takes a translator (ExportTranslator) and renders the whole
  note from a new `ehrExport` message namespace (42 keys, en + da).
- ICU plurals localise ("# week"/"# weeks" -> "# uge"/"# uger"), as do week
  markers (W -> uge), units (units -> enheder), side codes (L/R/B -> V/H/B),
  guidance methods, modality labels, and the GAS/NRS goal sentence.
- The call site (clinician/patient) passes useTranslations('ehrExport').
- All the audit-fixes correctness behaviour is preserved (wearing-off guard,
  sustained gap-break, NRS-direction note, units reconciliation, switch
  defaults).
