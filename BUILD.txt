BUILD: video-score-queue  (Pass C of the video/baseline work)
=========================================================================
WHAT THIS BUILD DOES
A per-visit "Score videos" queue that clears the unscored peak-effect clips in
one pass. Each clip is shown beside the goal's baseline; the clinician scores
it and the queue auto-advances to the next.
- GAS goals: scored against the goal's anchors on −2..+2 (existing field).
- NRS goals: scored on the SAME 0–10 scale the patient uses, so you get a
  clinician-0–10 vs patient-0–10 comparison on one axis (new field).
