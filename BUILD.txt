BUILD: clinic-video-scoring
=========================================================================
WHAT THIS BUILD DOES
Adds CLINIC-SIDE STRUCTURED SCORING of the standardized check-in videos —
slice 2 of "separate capturing from judging". Slice 1 (guided-capture) made
the capture standardized; this makes the judging consistent: the clinician
scores each clip on the goal's GAS levels (-2..2), producing an
authoritative outcome rated by ONE consistent assessor (the clinic), which
stays comparable week to week even as the at-home informant rotates. An
"off-protocol / unusable" mark excludes a clip from that series.

Additive: a video with no score reads as "pending".

CHANGES
- NEW supabase/migrations/0072_clinic_video_score.sql:
    * weekly_goal_rating gains clinic_video_rating (-2..2),
      clinic_video_unusable, clinic_video_scored_by, clinic_video_scored_at.
    * set_clinic_video_score(rating, score, unusable) security-definer RPC
      (same access check as the other goal-video RPCs; "unusable" clears the

- components/clinician/VisitChanges.tsx: the per-clip play buttons now show a
  score badge (Clinic +1 / Unusable / Not scored) and open the scoring view.
- lib/supabase/clinicianPatient.ts: rating id + clinic score threaded into
  the clinician check-in data; new useSetClinicVideoScore hook.
- i18n: clinician.video.score.* (en + da). Parity 1097 == 1097.

HOW TO CONFIRM IT WORKED (needs a real browser + Supabase + a recorded
clip — cannot be tested in this environment)
- As a clinician with a patient who recorded a standardized clip, open it
  from the "since last visit" summary.
- Pick a GAS level: a GAS goal should show the goal's own anchor text on the
  buttons; an NRS goal should show the generic level meanings. Save, and
  confirm the clip's badge updates to "Clinic +/-n".
- Mark another clip "unusable" and confirm the badge shows Unusable and the
  numeric score is cleared.
- Negative check: a clinician without an active session for that patient
  cannot score (the RPC denies it).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO (see HANDOVER section 8)
- It does not yet CHART the clinic-scored series as its own trend line
  alongside the at-home ratings — the scores are stored and shown per clip
  as badges. The graph is the natural next step (slice 3 / visualization).
- It does not let you edit a video protocol on an existing goal (protocol is
  set at goal creation, since video is only enabled there).
