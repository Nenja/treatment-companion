WHAT'S NEW IN THIS BUILD
A language picker, so people can use the app in English, Danish, Swedish,
or Norwegian:
- On the LOGIN screen: a small EN / DA / SV / NB switch at the top. Tap
  one and the page redraws in that language (there's no account yet at
  that point, so it just changes the language on screen).
- In SETTINGS (profile page), just above Appearance: four buttons —
  English, Dansk, Svenska, Norsk. Tapping one switches the language AND
  saves it to the account. Like the colour/appearance settings, it
  applies straight away and is NOT tied to the Save button, so it never
  triggers the "unsaved changes" warning.

One honest note: the saved language is stored, but the app doesn't yet
auto-switch a returning user into it at sign-in (it currently follows
whatever language the login page was in). That's a small follow-up if
you want it — tell me and I'll add it.
