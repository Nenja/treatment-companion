WHAT IT DOES:
  A logged-in clinician/physiotherapist could otherwise try unlimited visit codes to guess one.
  Now the unlock blocks a user after 10 WRONG codes within 15 minutes ("Too many incorrect attempts —
  wait a few minutes"). Correct unlocks are never affected, so opening several patients in a row is
  fine. (Real codes are already single-use and expire quickly; this is extra defence.)

VERIFIED (this end): logic tested on a local database across 6 scenarios (valid single-use, reusable,
  wrong code, the 10-strike block, automatic unblock after 15 min, and that successful unlocks never
  trigger the block); the full migration set (now 99) applies cleanly from scratch; build 62/62;
  type-check clean; English/Danish text aligned.
