  Cumulative snapshot on top of redcap-automation-1. Removes the dev scenario
  launcher. No DB migration. Migrations still 0001-0110.

WHY
  The dev scenario launcher signed the user out (supabase.auth.signOut()) and
  then tried to sign back in via a magic-link token; on an environment without
  the expected demo accounts it failed AFTER signing out, leaving you on the
  login screen ("bounces to login even when logged in"). It had no real use, so
  it's retired.
