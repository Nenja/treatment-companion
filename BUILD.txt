BUILD: nrs-baseline-target  (Pass A of the video/baseline work)
=========================================================================
WHAT THIS BUILD DOES
NRS goals now have a baseline and a target (0–10), agreed with the patient in
clinic when the goal is recorded. This makes the weekly 0–10 self-report
interpretable ("started at 8, aiming for 4"). It is independent of video — the
pain/sleep example that motivated it isn't a video goal.

The "higher/lower is better" question is gone; direction is derived from
baseline vs target.

CHANGES
- Migration 0074: approved_goal gains nrs_baseline_value + nrs_target_value
  (int 0–10, nullable). create_goal_for_patient takes the two new values; the
  old 9-arg signature is dropped (single definition). Body otherwise identical
  to 0035.
- Record-goal form (NRS): two 0–10 inputs (now / target) replace the direction
  buttons; direction is computed (target >= baseline => higher-is-better).
- Goal graph: a faint "start" line and a dashed sage "target" line on the NRS
  chart, so the weekly dots read as a journey between them (also in the
  enlarged modal). GAS goals and the clinic-video chart are unchanged.
- No change to existing goals (null baseline/target => no lines drawn).
- i18n: newGoal.{nrsRangeLabel,nrsRangeHelp,nrsBaselineLabel,nrsTargetLabel} +
  treatment.{nrsStartTick,nrsTargetTick} (en + da). Parity 1126 == 1126.
