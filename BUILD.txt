BUILD: audit-followups   (remaining patient-audit items)
=========================================================================
NO MIGRATION — client/copy only.

CONTEXT
Most remaining audit items were already handled in the code (autoComplete +
inline password hint on signup; plain-language importance options; the check-in
already shows a named per-goal progress strip; pre-visit suggestions, status
echo and check-in undo shipped in earlier builds). This build does the
genuinely-open ones:

WHAT THIS BUILD DOES
- Signup: a patient-only note under the role choice setting expectations — your
  clinic connects your record at your first visit, and you can start suggesting
  goals before then.
- Onboarding: the patient wizard now teaches the visit code BEFORE the check-in
  (intro -> details -> visit -> checkin -> comfort) — connect first, then learn
  the weekly rating.
- Home: a gentle reassurance line under the goals — progress is uneven and a
  flat week isn't failure; the clinician looks at the whole picture. Shown only
  when the patient has goals.

DEPLOY
- Unzip; push to GitHub; Vercel builds. Page count 60. No SQL.

** QA (please verify — can't be tested here) **
- Signup shows the patient note for "Patient" and not for "Therapist".
- Onboarding step order reads visit -> check-in.
- The reassurance line appears under the goals when the patient has any.

NOT CHANGED (by design / can't action here)
- Clinic->patient visibility of clinician edits: intentionally upward-only.
- Visit-code 10-min expiry / no-screen fallback: inherent / clinic-side.
- Notifications push pipeline: opt-in UI exists; runtime/infra, untestable here.
- Visual focus-ring / contrast / screen-reader checks: need a rendered build;
  tap targets and aria-labels look correct in code.
