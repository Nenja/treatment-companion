Reverts + layout, from the latest review. UI-only; NO migration:

Face map:
- Dose dots reverted to the ORIGINAL colour ramp; the printed dose number
  on each mark is removed (on-screen and in the exported PNG). Symbol-mode
  marks reverted to their original backing. (Trade-off below.)
- The "+ Add a mark" button is removed (it added a mark at a default spot,
  which made little sense on a tap surface). Tapping the face still adds a
  mark; tapping a mark still edits/removes it.

Treatment page:
- No longer stretched: the single column is now capped at the mid width
  (720px) instead of expanding to 1080px. Mobile is unchanged.
- The "Treatment areas" selector and the "Last treatment" card now sit
  SIDE BY SIDE at the top of the form (stacked on mobile).

Brand on every page:
- A slim brand strip (the sage chevron + "Treatment Companion") now renders
  once in the layout, so it appears at the top of EVERY page. To avoid
  showing the brand twice, it was removed from the old per-page spots: the
  TopBar (used by the front page + patient-info) now carries only the
  account/help controls, and the text wordmark was removed from login,
  signup, and the clinician home.

A11y trade-offs reopened by the reverts (by request; flagged honestly):
- Dose-by-colour alone is not reliably distinguishable across 5 bands and
  no longer has the printed-number fallback. Exact dose is still shown when
  you tap a mark, the legend keys each band, and Symbol mode remains as the
  colour-independent option.
- Removing the add button means there is no keyboard-only way to CREATE a
  mark (editing existing marks stays keyboard-accessible).
