WHAT THE ADJUSTMENT LOOP DOES (web, normal GitHub -> Vercel)
A physiotherapist can flag a goal as "needs adjustment." Until now you saw
that flag but couldn't close it, so it lingered. Now each adjustment request
shows two buttons - "Mark addressed" and "Dismiss" - in both places you see
them (the patient cockpit and the treatment-page therapist panel). Tapping
either resolves it and it drops off your open list.

This is OPTION A you chose: clinician-side only. The therapist is NOT shown
the outcome (no new clinic->therapist channel); the status, time and who
resolved it are stored only for audit.
