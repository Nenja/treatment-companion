WHAT CHANGED IN THE EHR EXPORT (text)
The export is now a tight clinician-to-clinician handover. It drops the
patient name and the verbatim patient comments (both already in the EHR)
and keeps just the two things the EHR doesn't have:

1. TREATMENT — one line with product, total dose, dilution and guidance,
   then the muscles as "R gastrocnemius — 50 U" (face marks prefixed
   "face:"), the session note if any, and a check line if the listed
   doses don't add up to the recorded total. Doses use the clinical
   abbreviation (U in English, E in Danish/Swedish/Norwegian). The date
   sits in the header now, so it isn't repeated.

2. GOALS & RESPONSE —
   - NRS goals:  Baseline NRS 8 -> target NRS 4  (0-10, 10 = worst)
                 Best NRS 3 (wk7) - wearing off from wk10 - end NRS 5 (wk12)
   - GAS goals:  Best (wk5): better than expected - "<the level you wrote>"
                 End of cycle (wk12): as expected - wearing off from wk10
   GAS results are written in plain words (much worse ... much better than
   expected) with the actual outcome you described, so a clinician who has
   never seen this patient understands the result without needing the
   goal's setup. Wearing-off is included for both.
