WHAT CHANGED, IN PLAIN TERMS
  - The review screen now shows TWO buttons instead of five:
      * "Approve as a goal"  (primary)
      * "Set aside"          (secondary)
    There is no "discuss at next visit" button: a suggestion you don't act on
    stays pending and comes back at the next visit on its own.
  - "Approve as a goal" opens the form with a choice at the top:
      * "As a new goal"            -> the usual NRS/GAS setup (unchanged)
      * "Add to an existing goal"  -> pick one of the patient's current goals;
                                      it just RECORDS that this suggestion was
                                      folded into that goal. No new goal is
                                      created and the goal you pick is NOT
                                      changed.
