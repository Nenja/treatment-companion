WHAT CHANGED
------------
Usability rework of the clinician "Since last visit" card.

Before: it led with a bare "Weeks checked in: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5" list, then showed each
goal's movement as "5 -> 7" (which hid any dip in between).

Now:
- Goal movement is the headline. Each goal is a row with a small inline trend line
  (a dot on the current point), the current value emphasised (NRS "7/10"; GAS the
  descriptive level like "As expected"), and a change chip: an arrow + magnitude
  ("from 3" for NRS, "2 levels" for GAS, or "no change"). Green/up = improved,
  amber/down = declined, and it's direction-aware (a lower-is-better goal going down
  reads as improved). Colour is never the only signal (arrow + screen-reader text).
- The check-in list became a count ("5 check-ins") plus an adherence note in the
  subline ("checked in every week", or "missed week 3" when there's a gap).
- Training shows a cadence ("Home 14 days - ~3x/wk") and therapist sessions, in a
  compact strip along the bottom, with the video count when present.

Only components/clinician/VisitChanges.tsx changed; its translation namespace
(visitChanges, en + da) was updated to match. No database or data-layer change.

DEPLOY STEPS
------------
1. Unzip and push the contents to your GitHub repo (replace the working tree).
2. Vercel builds & deploys automatically on push.
3. No migrations, no new environment variables.

HOW TO CONFIRM
--------------
1. Open a patient with check-ins this cycle (e.g. test1). The "Since last visit"
   card now shows each goal on its own line with a small trend line, the current
   value, and a coloured change chip.
2. The hand goal (NRS) shows e.g. "7/10" with an up arrow and "from 3"; the standing
   goal (GAS) shows its current level with "2 levels".
3. Below the goals: a check-in count, an adherence note, and a compact strip for
   training and any video.
